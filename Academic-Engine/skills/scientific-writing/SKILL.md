# Scientific & Technical Writing

## Purpose
Guides the drafting and editing of academic manuscripts, technical reports, methodology chapters, and system specifications with academic rigor and proper citations.

## When to Use
- Writing academic papers or course reports
- Structuring methodology and results sections
- Integrating citations according to APA 7 or IEEE
- Editing for formal tone and active voice
